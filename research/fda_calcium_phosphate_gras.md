# FDA/GRAS Status of Calcium Phosphates (E341) – Research Findings

## Summary
Calcium phosphates (mono-, di-, and tribasic) are **GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe)** under multiple 21 CFR sections, with no quantitative limits—only the requirement to follow Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP). They also have specific approved uses as a color additive with defined limits.

---

Authority: 21 U.S.C. 321, 342, 348, 371

## Color Additive Listing (21 CFR Part 73)

### § 73.80 — Calcium phosphate (Color Additive)
- **URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73/subpart-A/section-73.80
- **Identity:** White, synthetically prepared powder consisting predominantly of precipitated Ca₅OH(PO₄)₃.
- **Specifications:**
  - Loss on ignition: not more than 10%
  - Assay (Ca): 36.0–40.0%
  - Fluoride: not more than 75 mg/kg (75 ppm)
  - Lead: not more than 0.25 mg/kg (0.25 ppm)
  - Arsenic: not more than 3 mg/kg (3 ppm)
- **Uses and Restrictions (specific limits when used as a colorant):**
  1. Ready-to-eat chicken products: not exceeding **1.5%** by weight of finished food
  2. White candy melts: not exceeding **0.25%** by weight of finished food
  3. Doughnut sugar: not exceeding **2.0%** by weight of finished food
  4. Sugar for coated candies: not exceeding **5.25%** by weight of finished food
- **Labeling:** Must conform to § 70.25 requirements.
- **Certification:** Exempt from certification (not necessary for public health protection).
- **Date:** [90 FR 20100, May 12, 2025]

## Specific Food Standard Uses

### § 137.185 — Enriched Self-Rising Flour
- **URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-137/subpart-B/section-137.185
- Dicalcium phosphate is specifically mentioned: "When calcium is added as dicalcium phosphate, such dicalcium phosphate is also considered to be an acid-reacting substance."
- Required calcium content: 960 mg per pound of flour.

## General GRAS Framework (21 CFR § 182.1)
- **URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-182/subpart-A/section-182.1
- **Good Manufacturing Practice** means:
  1. Quantity does not exceed amount reasonably required for intended physical, nutritional, or technical effect
  2. Incidental amounts reduced to extent reasonably possible
  3. Substance must be of appropriate food grade, prepared and handled as a food ingredient
- Substances may be reevaluated and moved to Part 184 ("affirmed as GRAS"), food additive regulations (Parts 170–180), interim regulations (Part 180), or prohibited (Part 189).

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **All three forms** (monobasic/monocalcium phosphate, dibasic/dicalcium phosphate, and tribasic/tricalcium phosphate) are GRAS.
2. **No numeric use limits** for general food use — only GMP restrictions apply.
3. **Multiple functional categories:** multipurpose food substance, sequestrant, nutrient, color additive, and stabilizer in food packaging.
4. **As a color additive** (§ 73.80), specific quantitative limits apply (0.25%–5.25% depending on food category).
5. **As a food packaging stabilizer** (§ 181.29), it is a prior-sanctioned substance.
6. The GRAS status applies under the authority of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, section 409 (21 U.S.C. 348).
