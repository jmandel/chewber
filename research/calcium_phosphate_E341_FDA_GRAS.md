# FDA GRAS Status: Calcium Phosphates (E341)

## Research Date: 2025-06-26
## Source: eCFR (Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21)

---

## SUMMARY

All three forms of calcium phosphate (monobasic, dibasic, and tribasic) are **FDA GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe)** for human food use under **21 CFR Part 182**. They are listed under multiple subparts covering different functional uses. There are **NO separate Part 184 sections** for calcium phosphate — the originally suggested §184.1185 and §184.1187 are actually Calcium acetate and Calcium alginate, respectively.

Additionally, calcium phosphate (tribasic) is listed as a **color additive exempt from certification** under 21 CFR 73.80 (new rule effective May 2025).

### 4. 21 CFR §73.80 — Calcium Phosphate (Color Additive)

- **URL**: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73/subpart-A/section-73.80
- **Part**: 73 (Listing of Color Additives Exempt from Certification)
- **Subpart**: A — Foods
- **Product**: Calcium phosphate — white, synthetically prepared powder consisting predominantly of precipitated Ca₅OH(PO₄)₃ (tribasic/hydroxyapatite form)
- **Status**: ✅ Approved color additive, exempt from certification
- **Date**: [90 FR 20100, May 12, 2025] — recently finalized rule
- **Specifications**:
  - Loss on ignition: not more than 10%
  - Assay (Ca): 36.0-40.0%
  - Fluoride: not more than 75 mg/kg (75 ppm)
  - Lead: not more than 0.25 mg/kg (0.25 ppm)
  - Arsenic: not more than 3 mg/kg (3 ppm)
- **Uses and Restrictions** (SPECIFIC LIMITS):
  - Ready-to-eat chicken products: not exceeding 1.5% by weight of finished food
  - White candy melts: not exceeding 0.25% by weight of finished food
  - Doughnut sugar: not exceeding 2.0% by weight of finished food
  - Sugar for coated candies: not exceeding 5.25% by weight of finished food
- **Labeling**: Must conform to §70.25 requirements

## CORRECTION ON ORIGINALLY SUGGESTED SECTION NUMBERS

| Suggested Section | Actual Content | Correct Section for Calcium Phosphate |
|---|---|---|
| §184.1185 | **Calcium acetate** (NOT calcium phosphate) | N/A — No Part 184 listing exists |
| §184.1187 | **Calcium alginate** (NOT calcium phosphate) | N/A — No Part 184 listing exists |
| §182.1217 | ✅ Calcium phosphate (mono-, di-, tribasic) | Correct |

**Key finding**: Calcium phosphate is NOT listed in Part 184 (Direct food substances affirmed as GRAS). It is listed in **Part 182** (Substances generally recognized as safe), which is a slightly different regulatory pathway. Part 182 substances were GRAS prior to 1958 and are listed without the detailed specifications/use-level requirements that Part 184 substances have.
