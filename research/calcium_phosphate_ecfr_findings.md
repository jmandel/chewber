# Calcium Phosphate - eCFR (FDA) Regulatory Findings

Searched: eCFR Title 21 (Food and Drugs) for "calcium phosphate"
Date of regulations: Up to date as of 2/25/2026

## Summary

Calcium phosphate appears in **5 distinct FDA regulatory sections** across Title 21 CFR:
- 3 sections in Part 182 (GRAS for human food)
- 1 section in Part 73 (Color additive)
- 2 sections in Part 582 (GRAS for animal feed - not detailed below)

**Calcium phosphate is NOT listed in Part 184** (Direct Food Substances Affirmed as GRAS).

## Section 4: 21 CFR § 73.80 — Calcium phosphate (Color Additive)

**URL:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73/subpart-A/section-73.80

- **Part:** 73 — Listing of Color Additives Exempt from Certification
- **Subpart:** A — Foods
- **Status:** Approved color additive (exempt from certification)
- **Identity:** White, synthetically prepared powder consisting predominantly of precipitated Ca5OH(PO4)3
- **Specifications:**
  - Loss on ignition: not more than 10%
  - Assay (Ca): 36.0–40.0%
  - Fluoride: not more than 75 mg/kg (75 ppm)
  - Lead: not more than 0.25 mg/kg (0.25 ppm)
  - Arsenic: not more than 3 mg/kg (3 ppm)
- **Uses and restrictions (specific limits):**
  - Ready-to-eat chicken products: not exceeding 1.5% by weight
  - White candy melts: not exceeding 0.25% by weight
  - Doughnut sugar: not exceeding 2.0% by weight
  - Sugar for coated candies: not exceeding 5.25% by weight
- **Labeling:** Must conform to § 70.25
- **Certification exemption:** Exempt from certification under section 721(c) of the FD&C Act
- **Citation:** [90 FR 20100, May 12, 2025]

## Key Takeaways for Food Products

1. **Calcium phosphate (mono-, di-, and tribasic) is GRAS** under 21 CFR Part 182 for human food use.
2. It is listed under **three functional categories**: multiple purpose food substance (§182.1217), sequestrant (§182.6215 — monobasic only), and nutrient (§182.8217).
3. All GRAS listings require use "in accordance with good manufacturing practice" — no specific numeric limits for GRAS use.
4. When used as a **color additive** (§73.80), specific concentration limits apply per food type.
5. It is **not** in Part 184 (Direct Food Substances Affirmed as GRAS through petition process).
