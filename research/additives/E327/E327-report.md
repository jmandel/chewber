# E327 (Calcium lactate) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E327
- **Name:** Calcium lactate
- **CAS numbers identified:** 814-80-2 (anhydrous calcium lactate), 5743-48-6 (hydrated form listed in multiple databases)
- **Chemical class:** Calcium salt of lactic acid (organic acid salt; acidity regulator/mineral salt)
- **Common synonyms:** Calcium dilactate; calcium 2-hydroxypropanoate; calcium bis(2-hydroxypropanoate)
- **Natural vs synthetic:** **Semi-synthetic/commercially manufactured** (prepared by neutralizing lactic acid with calcium carbonate or calcium hydroxide); lactic acid precursor can be fermentation-derived.

Evidence:
- 21 CFR 184.1207 identifies calcium lactate and CAS 814-80-2 and describes commercial preparation route. [S4]
- EFSA OpenEFSA substance record lists calcium lactate as E327 and includes CAS 5743-48-6 and EC 227-266-2. [S2]
- PubChem record for calcium lactate (CID 13144) returns formula C6H10CaO6 and IUPAC calcium bis(2-hydroxypropanoate), including both CAS numbers as synonyms. [S8][S9]

## Function in Food
- **Primary technological role:** Acidity regulator / firming agent.
- **Other technological roles (U.S. GRAS regulation):** flavor enhancer, flavoring agent/adjuvant, leavening agent, nutrient supplement, stabilizer/thickener.
- **Mechanism of action:**
  - Buffers/acidity-adjusts formulations via lactate anion/calcium salt chemistry.
  - Provides calcium fortification.
  - Improves texture/firmness in selected products via ionic interactions with food matrices.
- **Common food categories:** broad GMP-based use in processed foods; CFR defines use as direct human food ingredient under GMP, excluding infant foods and infant formulas.

Evidence:
- 21 CFR 184.1207(c)(1)-(2) lists functional classes and GMP conditions of use (with infant-food/formula exclusion). [S4]
- WHO JECFA API listing for calcium lactate includes functional classes such as acidity regulator and flour treatment agent. [S5]

## Key Safety Evidence
### Animal studies
- **Chronic study (PMID: 1937289, 1991):** two-year F344 rat study in drinking water (up to 5%) reported no clear treatment-specific toxicity and no significant dose-related tumor increase.
- **Subchronic study (PMID: 2636938, 1989):** 13-week F344 rat study up to 5% in drinking water reported no severe toxicological findings.

### Additional toxicology signals
- **Acute rat study (PMID: 24191319, 2013)** on a calcium lactate-gluconate/polycan mixture found no treatment-related mortality up to 2000 mg/kg in a single-dose design.
- **In vitro study (PMID: 36536829, 2023)** reported reduced HUVEC viability for calcium lactate only at higher concentrations; relevance to normal dietary exposure is uncertain.

### Regulatory toxicology context
- **EFSA FEEDAP paper indexed in PubMed (PMID: 32625591)** addresses lactic acid/calcium lactate in animal feed use scenarios and identifies species-specific tolerance constraints, not direct human-food ADI setting.

### Epidemiological data
- No robust human epidemiological studies directly linking typical dietary calcium lactate intake to major chronic disease outcomes were identified in this search.

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis
**risk_free**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - JECFA ADI is “NOT LIMITED.”
  - No IARC carcinogenic classification identified.
  - Animal chronic data do not show carcinogenic signal.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - EFSA E327 re-evaluation remains ongoing without a finalized current output.
  - Infant-use cautions/restrictions appear in both U.S. and JECFA sources.

**limited**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - FDA GRAS status with explicit CFR conditions.
  - No strong human or animal evidence of serious harm at standard food-use levels.
  - JECFA ADI not limited supports low concern under intended use.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - Incomplete contemporary EU finalization (ongoing EFSA re-evaluation) adds uncertainty.

**moderate**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - Presence of subgroup-specific cautions (infants; D-/DL-lactate context).
  - EFSA re-evaluation unresolved as of latest record.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - No major regulatory body reviewed here has moved to ban/withdraw.
  - No compelling carcinogenic/genotoxic population-level evidence identified for normal use.

**high**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - None identified.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - No IARC Group 1/2A/2B listing found for calcium lactate.
  - FDA GRAS status remains in place; JECFA ADI remains not limited.

### 2. Rationale
The dataset supports a **low overall risk under regulated food use**, but not a pure `risk_free` assignment because EFSA’s dedicated E327 re-evaluation remains ongoing and infant-related caution exists in both U.S. and JECFA records.

### 3. Recommended tier
**limited**
