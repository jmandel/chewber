# E341 — Calcium Phosphates

## Summary

Calcium phosphates (E341) are a group of calcium salts of phosphoric acid used
widely in food production as acidity regulators, anti-caking agents, emulsifiers,
and raising agents.

## Variants

- **E341(i)** — Monocalcium phosphate (MCP)
- **E341(ii)** — Dicalcium phosphate (DCP)
- **E341(iii)** — Tricalcium phosphate (TCP)

## Safety Profile

Generally recognized as safe (GRAS) by the FDA. EFSA re-evaluated phosphates
in 2019 and established a group ADI of 40 mg/kg bw/day expressed as phosphorus.

## Concerns

Excessive dietary phosphate intake is associated with cardiovascular
calcification and kidney function impairment, particularly in populations
with pre-existing renal conditions.
