# E380 (Triammonium citrate) Research Report

## Identity

- **E-number:** E380 (INS 380)  
  Source: WHO/JECFA chemical entry for Triammonium citrate (1979 evaluation history), https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504
- **Primary name:** Triammonium citrate  
  Source: WHO/JECFA chemical entry, https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504
- **CAS number(s):**
  - **3458-72-8** (Triammonium citrate / ammonium citrate tribasic)
  - **3012-65-5** (related U.S. CFR entry for **ammonium citrate, dibasic**; not explicitly triammonium citrate)
  Sources: JECFA entry (CAS 3458-72-8), https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504; eCFR API text for 21 CFR 184.1140 (CAS 3012-65-5), https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-19/title-21.xml?part=184
- **Chemical class:** Ammonium salt(s) of citric acid (citrate salt; acid/base buffering agent).  
  Sources: JECFA identity/synonyms and function fields, https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504; eCFR section text describing ammonium citrate dibasic as a citric-acid salt, https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-19/title-21.xml?part=184
- **Common synonyms:**
  - Citric acid triammonium salt
  - Ammonium citrate tribasic
  - Triammonium salt of 2-hydroxypropane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid
  Source: JECFA chemical entry, https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504
- **Natural vs synthetic:** **Synthetic (manufactured salt)**. Triammonium citrate is an added citrate salt; related CFR ammonium citrate salt is described as prepared by neutralizing citric acid with ammonia.  
  Source: eCFR section 184.1140 preparation description (for dibasic ammonium citrate), https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-19/title-21.xml?part=184

## Function in Food

- **Primary technological role:** Acidity regulator / pH control, with buffering behavior from citrate/ammonium ions.  
  Source: JECFA functional class = ACIDITY_REGULATOR, https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/504
- **Related roles in U.S. CFR for ammonium citrate dibasic:** Flavor enhancer and pH control agent.  
  Source: 21 CFR 184.1140(c)(1), eCFR API full text, https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-19/title-21.xml?part=184
- **Common food categories (explicitly listed in CFR for the related dibasic salt):** Nonalcoholic beverages and cheeses, at levels not exceeding current good manufacturing practice (cGMP).  
  Source: 21 CFR 184.1140(c)(2), https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-19/title-21.xml?part=184

## Risk Assessment

### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis

#### risk_free

- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - JECFA ADI is “NOT LIMITED,” and triammonium citrate is grouped under citric-acid-and-bases ADI logic.
  - Functional role is standard acidity regulation.
- **Evidence against this tier:**
  - EFSA re-evaluation table shows no completed EFSA opinion entry for E380 (no adoption/publication date/link in row), so current EU scientific re-evaluation closure is not visible from that table.
  - Direct modern toxicology literature specific to E380 is sparse in PubMed search outputs.

#### limited

- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - Authorized in EU permitted-additives status table.
  - Related ammonium citrate (dibasic) has GRAS affirmation in 21 CFR 184.1140 under cGMP.
  - JECFA ADI “NOT LIMITED” supports low concern at intended use levels.
  - No IARC listing identified for this specific chemical.
- **Evidence against this tier:**
  - U.S. CFR section is for dibasic ammonium citrate, not a named triammonium-citrate section.
  - EFSA re-evaluation completeness for E380 is unresolved in the currently posted status table.

#### moderate

- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - Data-gap signals: no completed EFSA re-evaluation opinion entry in the status table, limited direct modern E380 toxicology.
- **Evidence against this tier:**
  - No clear regulatory restriction trend, no IARC carcinogenic classification signal for this compound, and JECFA ADI remains “NOT LIMITED.”

#### high

- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - None identified from retrieved EFSA/eCFR/JECFA/IARC/PubMed evidence.
- **Evidence against this tier:**
  - No major-jurisdiction ban identified, no IARC Group 2A/2B/1 assignment found for triammonium citrate, and no strong mechanistic or epidemiological harm signal from the retrieved evidence.

### 2. Rationale

The strongest anchors are JECFA’s “NOT LIMITED” ADI and lack of carcinogenic classification signal in IARC listings for this substance. The main uncertainty is not a positive hazard signal but a **data recency/completeness gap** (EFSA row not showing completed re-evaluation opinion entry; sparse direct modern toxicology papers specifically on E380).

### 3. Recommended tier

**Recommended tier: `limited`**

This reflects generally low-risk regulatory posture with unresolved data-completeness issues rather than demonstrated moderate/high harm.
