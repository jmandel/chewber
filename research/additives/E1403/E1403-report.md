# E1403 Research Report (Bleached starch)

## Identity
- **E-number:** E1403
- **Name:** Bleached starch
- **INS number:** 1403 (listed in JECFA synonyms under the modified starches group entry)
- **CAS number(s):** No unique CAS number for E1403 was found in the retrieved EFSA/FDA/JECFA records. The JECFA **modified starches** group page lists CAS numbers for some related derivatives (e.g., starch acetate, acetylated distarch adipate, hydroxypropyl starch, hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate), but not a dedicated CAS for E1403 itself.
- **Chemical class:** Chemically modified starch (food additive class)
- **Common synonyms:** "INS No. 1403 Bleached starch", "food starch-modified" (U.S. CFR umbrella term)
- **Natural vs synthetic:** **semi-synthetic** (derived from native starch, then chemically processed)

Evidence: JECFA modified starches profile and FDA 21 CFR 172.892 text.[S1][S2]

## Function in Food
- **Mechanism of action:** Bleaching/chemical treatment of starch modifies appearance and functional behavior; in FDA rules this is part of "food starch-modified" processing and is controlled by reagent-specific limits.
- **Common technological roles:** Thickener, stabilizer, emulsifier (JECFA functional classes for modified starches).
- **Common food categories:** Broad processed-food use as "food starch-modified" under GMP/limit conditions; one CFR limitation explicitly restricts calcium-hypochlorite bleached starch to batter for commercially processed foods.

Evidence: JECFA functional class and FDA section-level use conditions.[S1][S2]

### FDA / CFR (US)
- **CFR citation:** **21 CFR 172.892 (Food starch-modified)**.
- **Status in CFR framework:** approved as a direct food additive category (Part 172: "Food Additives Permitted for Direct Addition to Food for Human Consumption").
- **GRAS status (as requested):** no standalone GRAS affirmation for "bleached starch" as such was identified in the retrieved CFR records; the operative entry is Part 172 food-additive authorization.
- **Conditions of use (examples from 172.892(b)):** bleaching may use listed reagents with limits (e.g., hydrogen peroxide/peracetic acid active oxygen <=0.45%; ammonium persulfate <=0.075% with sulfur dioxide <=0.05%; sodium chlorite <=0.5%; etc.). A specific limitation is present for chlorine as calcium hypochlorite treatment (finished product limited to batter for commercially processed foods).

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis
**risk_free**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - JECFA ADI "NOT SPECIFIED" for modified starches including INS 1403.[S1]
  - No IARC listing found for E1403-related terms in the current classification dataset.[S7][S8]
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - No direct, modern EFSA E1403-specific re-evaluation was located.
  - PubMed evidence is sparse and mostly indirect for E1403.

**limited**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - U.S. authorization under 21 CFR 172.892 with explicit reagent/use limits.
  - JECFA ADI "NOT SPECIFIED" and no clear new hazard signal in retrieved regulatory data.
  - No IARC classification found.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - E1403-specific toxicology and exposure datasets are limited in the retrieved literature.

**moderate**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - Data-gap argument: additive-specific contemporary toxicology/intake evidence is thin.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - Retrieved regulatory sources do not show a reduced ADI, major restriction trend, or carcinogenic classification specific to E1403.

**high**
- Evidence supporting this tier:
  - None identified in the required-source sweep.
- Evidence arguing against this tier:
  - No major-jurisdiction ban identified in retrieved sources.
  - No IARC Group 1/2A/2B entry found for E1403 terms.
  - JECFA ADI remains "NOT SPECIFIED" for the group including INS 1403.

### 2. Rationale
The strongest direct signals are regulatory acceptance frameworks (FDA conditions of use; JECFA ADI not specified for the relevant modified-starch group), with no IARC signal found. The main uncertainty is evidence granularity: E1403-specific modern toxicology and exposure data were not robustly available from retrieved sources.

### 3. Recommended tier
**limited**
