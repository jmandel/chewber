# E503 (Ammonium carbonates) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E503 (E503(i) ammonium carbonate; E503(ii) ammonium hydrogen carbonate/ammonium bicarbonate) [4][5][8][10]
- **Chemical name:** Ammonium carbonates [4][5]
- **CAS number(s):** 8000-73-5 (21 CFR ammonium carbonate), 1066-33-7 (21 CFR/JECFA ammonium bicarbonate), 10361-29-2 (JECFA INS 503i entry) [7][8][10]
- **Chemical class:** Inorganic ammonium salts of carbonic acid (carbonate/bicarbonate system; 503i may include carbamate in varying proportions) [8][10]
- **Common synonyms:** Ammonium carbonate, ammonium hydrogen carbonate, ammonium bicarbonate, INS 503i, INS 503ii [8][10]
- **Natural vs synthetic:** Commercial food-grade material is manufactured (e.g., reaction/sublimation processes described in CFR/JECFA), so food-additive use is **synthetic** [7][8][10]

## Function in Food
- **Primary technological role:** Raising agent (leavening) [8][10]
- **Secondary roles:** Acidity regulator / pH control agent; dough strengthener (for bicarbonate entry in CFR) [7][8][10]
- **Mechanism of action:** Used as an alkali leavening system in doughs/batters and as a pH-adjusting agent in formulations [7][8]
- **Common food categories:** Bakery applications using chemical leavening (typical for ammonium carbonates/bicarbonates), under GMP-limited use frameworks [7][8]

### FDA / eCFR (Title 21)
- eCFR human-facing section pages were blocked in CLI by anti-bot challenge; eCFR developer API was used to retrieve the official Title 21 text [6][7].
- **GRAS status:** both ammonium bicarbonate and ammonium carbonate are in **21 CFR Part 184** (direct food substances affirmed as GRAS), with explicit language affirming GRAS under current good manufacturing practice [7].
- **CFR citations and conditions of use:**
  - **21 CFR 184.1135 (Ammonium bicarbonate):** GRAS with no limitation other than cGMP; used as dough strengthener, leavening agent, pH control agent, texturizer [7]
  - **21 CFR 184.1137 (Ammonium carbonate):** GRAS with no limitation other than cGMP; used as leavening agent and pH control agent [7]

## Key Safety Evidence
### Animal studies
- No recent, direct E503-specific oral animal toxicology studies were identified in the targeted PubMed searches run here [13][15].
- Historical toxicological basis for regulatory acceptance is reflected in JECFA’s longstanding “ADI not specified” outcome (1982 entry) [8][10].

### Epidemiological data
- No robust, additive-specific human epidemiology isolating E503 intake and adverse outcomes was identified in the PubMed queries performed here [13][15].

### Mechanistic concerns
- A 2025 Food Chemistry study (oral cell model) reported that sodium carbonate/ammonium bicarbonate can alter interactions between astringent compounds and oral constituents; this is mechanistic/in vitro work, not direct clinical toxicity evidence [14].

- **ADI exceedance risk:**

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis
- **risk_free**
  - Evidence supporting placement: JECFA ADI “not specified”; FDA GRAS affirmations under cGMP for ammonium bicarbonate/carbonate [7][8][10].
  - Evidence against placement: EFSA re-evaluation for E503 is still ongoing, with no finalized output available in retrieved EFSA records [4][5].

- **limited**
  - Evidence supporting placement: Longstanding approval/GRAS status in US CFR and permissive JECFA ADI basis; no IARC classification signal identified [7][8][10][11][12].
  - Evidence against placement: Data gap from not-yet-finalized EFSA re-evaluation; limited modern additive-specific human/animal safety literature [4][5][13].

- **moderate**
  - Evidence supporting placement: Ongoing EFSA re-evaluation and sparse contemporary additive-specific toxicology create uncertainty; one in vitro study indicates potential biochemical interaction effects [4][5][14].
  - Evidence against placement: No ban pattern, no IARC classification hit, and no strong adverse signal in retrieved regulatory toxicology endpoints [7][8][10][11][12].

- **high**
  - Evidence supporting placement: None identified from retrieved sources.
  - Evidence against placement: US GRAS affirmations, JECFA ADI not specified, absence of identified IARC classification, and no major-ban evidence [7][8][10][11][12].

### 2. Rationale
The weight of currently retrievable regulatory evidence supports low concern at normal food-use conditions (FDA GRAS + JECFA ADI not specified). However, EFSA’s E503 re-evaluation is still pending and explicitly delayed, so a fully up-to-date EU scientific conclusion is not yet finalized in the retrieved records.

### 3. Recommended tier
**Recommended tier: `limited`**
