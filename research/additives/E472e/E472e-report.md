# E472e (DATEM; diacetyl tartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number**: E472e (INS 472e). [S3][S8]
- **Name**: Mono- and diacetyltartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids (DATEM). [S3][S7][S8]
- **CAS number(s)**: No single CAS number was listed in the consulted WHO/JECFA record (`CAS_NO: null`), consistent with DATEM being a variable ester mixture rather than one discrete molecule. [S8][S9]
- **Chemical class**: Emulsifier mixture of glycerol esters with (di)acetyltartaric acid and fatty acids. [S3][S7]
- **Common synonyms**: DATEM; diacetyltartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides; INS No. 472e. [S7][S8]
- **Natural vs synthetic**: **Semi-synthetic** (manufactured from edible mono-/diglycerides and tartaric/acetic derivatives). [S3][S7]

## Function in Food
- **Mechanism of action**: Amphiphilic ester mixture that reduces oil-water interfacial tension and improves emulsion/dough stability. [S3][S7]
- **Common food categories**:
  - Bread and bread products, cakes/pastries/sweet goods, pizzas, sandwiches/wraps (high-frequency labeling categories in EFSA 2025 opinion). [S3]
  - In US GRAS regulation: baked goods and baking mixes, nonalcoholic beverages, confections/frostings, dairy analogs, fats and oils. [S7]

## Regulatory Status
- **EFSA (EU)**
  - **Latest EFSA opinion identified**: 2025 scientific opinion on specifications for E472a,b,d,e,f (EFSA Journal e9602). [S2][S3]
  - **Approval status**: Authorized food additive in EU; EFSA concluded no safety concern for assessed uses/specification changes in the 2025 opinion. [S3]
  - **ADI**:
    - EFSA 2025 reports ADI for **E472e = 600 mg/kg bw/day** (additive basis), with exposures below ADI in assessed scenarios. [S2][S3]
    - EFSA 2020 re-evaluation basis: group ADI **240 mg/kg bw/day expressed as tartaric acid** for E472d/e/f. [S4][S5]
  - **Evaluation year**: 2025 (latest EFSA scientific opinion located). [S2]
  - **Key conclusion**: Across population groups/scenarios considered, estimated exposure remained below the ADI for E472e. [S2][S3]
- **FDA / eCFR (US)**
  - **Status**: GRAS as a direct human food ingredient. [S7]
  - **CFR citation**: **21 CFR 184.1101** (Diacetyl tartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides). [S7]
  - **Conditions of use**: GMP-limited use as emulsifier/emulsifier salt and as flavoring agent/adjuvant in specified food categories; DATEM acronym allowed on labels. [S7]
- **JECFA / WHO**
  - **Entry**: DIACETYLTARTARIC and FATTY ACID ESTERS of GLYCEROL (includes INS 472e,f). [S8][S9]
  - **ADI**: **0-50 mg/kg bw**. [S8][S9]
  - **Last evaluation year shown in WHO/JECFA page**: **2003** (with prior 2001 temporary ADI history shown). [S8]
- **IARC**
  - No specific IARC Monographs classification entry found for DATEM/E472e in the IARC classification dataset checked. [S10][S11]
- **Notable bans**
  - No major-jurisdiction outright ban identified in EFSA/FDA/JECFA/IARC sources reviewed. [S2][S3][S7][S8][S10]

## Key Safety Evidence
- **Regulatory toxicology (directly relevant)**
  - EFSA 2020: no adverse effects relevant for humans were identified for E472a-f in the available toxicological database; E472e safety benchmarked via tartaric-acid-based ADI approach. [S4][S5]
  - EFSA 2025: exposure estimates for E472e remained below ADI; no safety concern under assessed uses/spec changes. [S2][S3]
- **Human epidemiology (recent)**
  - 2024 NutriNet-Sante prospective cohort (Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol): higher E472e intake associated with higher type 2 diabetes risk (HR 1.04 per +100 mg/day; observational). [S12]
  - 2023 NutriNet-Sante BMJ cohort reported CVD associations for E471/E472 groups and some individual emulsifiers; this is indirect for E472e-specific causality. [S13]
- **Mechanistic concerns**
  - Emulsifier literature supports plausible gut/microbial and metabolic pathways, but direct mechanistic causality specific to DATEM remains limited in humans. [S12][S13]

## Exposure Assessment
- **EFSA 2025 (E472e) quantitative exposure examples**
  - Annex II + III, refined non-brand-loyal scenario: mean up to **44.8 mg/kg bw/day** (toddlers), 95th percentile up to **87.5 mg/kg bw/day** (toddlers). [S3]
  - Annex II + III, maximum-level scenario: 95th percentile up to **279.7 mg/kg bw/day** (toddlers). [S3]
  - Annex II + III, FSMP scenario: 95th percentile up to **277.1 mg/kg bw/day** (children). [S3]
- **ADI exceedance risk**
  - EFSA states estimates were below **600 mg/kg bw/day** ADI for E472e. ADI exceedance was not identified in the assessed scenarios. [S2][S3]
- **Vulnerable populations**
  - Higher per-kg exposure groups are infants/toddlers/children and FSMP-related scenarios. [S3]

## Risk Assessment
1. **Tier-by-tier analysis**
- **risk_free**
  - Evidence supporting: No IARC classification found; EFSA reports exposure below ADI and no safety concern at assessed uses. [S2][S3][S10][S11]
  - Evidence against: A numerical ADI exists (not “ADI not specified”), and recent observational human signals suggest possible metabolic risk associations. [S2][S12]
- **limited**
  - Evidence supporting: EFSA-authorized with explicit ADI framework and below-ADI exposures; FDA GRAS status with defined CFR use conditions; JECFA ADI exists. [S2][S3][S7][S8]
  - Evidence against: Epidemiological associations (type 2 diabetes; broader emulsifier-CVD signals) create residual uncertainty, though causality is not established. [S12][S13]
- **moderate**
  - Evidence supporting: Credible human cohort signal for E472e and type 2 diabetes, plus class-level emulsifier concerns. [S12][S13]
  - Evidence against: Evidence is observational and potentially confounded; no regulatory withdrawal/ban and EFSA still finds below-ADI exposure and no current safety concern. [S2][S3]
- **high**
  - Evidence supporting: None substantial in reviewed sources.
  - Evidence against: No major bans identified, no IARC carcinogenic classification entry for DATEM, and regulatory assessments remain permissive with conditions. [S3][S7][S10][S11]

2. **Rationale**
- Current regulatory evidence is still predominantly reassuring for authorized use levels (EFSA 2025 below-ADI conclusion + FDA GRAS conditions).
- The main uncertainty comes from newer observational epidemiology (notably the 2024 NutriNet-Sante E472e association with type 2 diabetes), which is important but not causal proof.

3. **Recommended tier**
- **limited**
