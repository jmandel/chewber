# E900 (Dimethylpolysiloxane) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E900 (INS 900)
- **Primary name:** Dimethylpolysiloxane (also written dimethyl polysiloxane; polydimethylsiloxane)
- **CAS number(s) found in authoritative records:** 9016-00-6 (FDA inventory), 9006-65-9 and 63148-62-9 (JECFA toxicological summary)
- **Chemical class:** Organosilicon polymer (silicone; polysiloxane)
- **Common synonyms:** Polydimethylsiloxane, poly(methylsiloxane), dimethyl silicone, poly(oxy(dimethylsilylene))
- **Natural vs synthetic:** **Synthetic** (manufactured silicone polymer)

## Function in Food
- **Primary function:** Antifoaming/defoaming agent.
- **Mechanism of action:** Lowers surface tension and destabilizes foam films, reducing persistent foam during processing.
- **Common food categories (from CFR-linked uses):** General processed foods with a maximum finished-food level, plus specific listed contexts such as dry gelatin dessert mixes (with reconstitution limit) and salt labeled for cooking (with cooked-food limit); also referenced in standards such as pineapple juice processing.

## Regulatory Status
- **EFSA (EU):**
  - **Latest re-evaluation identified:** EFSA Journal 2020 (article 6107).
  - **Status:** Re-evaluated with conclusion of **no safety concern at reported uses and use levels**.
  - **ADI:** **17 mg/kg bw/day**.
  - **Evaluation year:** **2020**.
  - **Key conclusion details:** Very limited GI absorption, no genotoxicity concern, and exposure estimates across scenarios below the ADI.
  - **Access note:** The EFSA page redirects to Wiley; Wiley was challenge-blocked in this environment, so key values were extracted from the PubMed abstract of the same EFSA paper.

- **FDA / eCFR (US):**
  - **CFR citation:** **21 CFR 173.340** (defoaming agents).
  - **Status interpretation:** **Approved food additive use** under specific conditions (rather than a standalone GRAS listing in this section).
  - **Conditions of use (21 CFR 173.340):**
    - Dimethylpolysiloxane identity specifications are defined.
    - Maximum **10 ppm in food** (or equivalent in concentrates as consumed), with explicit exceptions including:
      - zero in milk,
      - 110 ppm in dry gelatin dessert mixes provided ready-to-serve dessert is <= 16 ppm,
      - 250 ppm in salt labeled for cooking provided cooked food is <= 10 ppm.

- **JECFA / WHO-FAO:**
  - **ADI:** **0-17 mg/kg bw/day**.
  - **Last evaluation year found:** **2011** (74th JECFA entry shown in FAO/JECFA detail records).

- **IARC:**
  - No dimethylpolysiloxane/polydimethylsiloxane entry was found on IARC Monographs "Agents Classified" resources checked.
  - **Classification outcome for this additive:** No specific IARC group classification identified in searched IARC resources.

- **Notable bans:**
  - No major jurisdiction-wide ban identified in the EFSA/FDA/JECFA/IARC sources reviewed.

## Key Safety Evidence
- **EFSA toxicology synthesis (2020):**
  - Very limited oral absorption; most excreted unchanged in feces.
  - No concern for genotoxicity.
  - Corneal findings in rats were interpreted as local contact effects (feed/fecal contact), not systemic toxicity.
  - From a 26-month rat study, NOAEL values cited were 1,742 mg/kg bw/day (female) and 2,055 mg/kg bw/day (male); EFSA derived ADI 17 mg/kg bw/day with uncertainty factor 100.
  - Overall conclusion: no safety concern at reported uses/use levels.

- **JECFA alignment:**
  - JECFA listing indicates ADI 0-17 mg/kg bw/day (2011 evaluation context), broadly consistent with EFSA's upward revision from older ADI values.

- **PubMed recency check:**
  - Additive-specific PubMed results are sparse; targeted title-level search for "dimethyl polysiloxane" + "food additive" returns the EFSA 2020 re-evaluation record.
  - Broad PDMS toxicity/safety searches return many non-food/material-science records, limiting direct new human dietary evidence.

- **Epidemiology:**
  - No strong additive-specific human epidemiological signal was identified in the reviewed PubMed searches.

## Exposure Assessment
- **Regulatory exposure benchmark:** ADI 17 mg/kg bw/day (EFSA) and 0-17 mg/kg bw/day (JECFA listing).
- **Estimated dietary exposure vs ADI:** EFSA reported exposure estimates across population scenarios did **not** exceed 17 mg/kg bw/day.
- **ADI exceedance risk:** Low based on EFSA's reported scenarios.
- **Potentially vulnerable populations:** Higher per-kg intake groups (e.g., children/high consumers) are generally the usual focus for screening, but EFSA's reported scenarios still remained below ADI.
- **Data gaps:** Limited recent additive-specific human studies in PubMed.

## Risk Assessment
1. **Tier-by-tier analysis**
- **risk_free**
  - Evidence supporting: low systemic absorption, no genotoxicity concern, and exposure below ADI in EFSA scenarios.
  - Evidence against: a numerical ADI is still required (not "ADI not specified"); historical toxicology findings required interpretation and safety depends on use limits.

- **limited**
  - Evidence supporting: approved in EU/US and evaluated by JECFA; clear ADI and CFR use limits; EFSA concluded no safety concern at reported uses.
  - Evidence against: additive-specific recent human evidence is limited; confidence relies heavily on regulatory dossier toxicology.

- **moderate**
  - Evidence supporting: could be argued if substantial real-world exceedance or robust new adverse data existed.
  - Evidence against: EFSA reported exposures below ADI, no strong modern regulatory signal of unresolved safety concern, and no major bans.

- **high**
  - Evidence supporting: none substantial in reviewed sources.
  - Evidence against: no IARC Group 2A/2B/1 signal for this additive, no EFSA/JECFA conclusion of unsafe at authorized uses, and no major jurisdictional bans identified.

2. **Rationale**
- The evidence base is dominated by regulatory evaluations showing low oral absorption, lack of genotoxic concern, ADI-setting with margin, and exposure estimates below ADI at reported uses. The main uncertainty is limited recent additive-specific human data rather than a strong hazard signal.

3. **Recommended tier**
- **limited**
