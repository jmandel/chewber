# E903 (Carnauba wax) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E903 (INS 903).
- **Name:** Carnauba wax.
- **CAS number(s):** 8015-86-9 (JECFA); eCFR section text shows CAS Reg. No. "008-015-869" (formatting variant of 8015-86-9).
- **Chemical class:** Natural plant wax; described as a mixture dominated by long-chain (C24-C32) saturated monofunctional fatty acids and primary alcohols.
- **Common synonyms:** Carnauba wax, INS 903, wax from Brazilian wax palm (*Copernicia cerifera*).
- **Natural vs synthetic:** Natural (obtained from leaves/buds of Brazilian wax palm).

Evidence:
- eCFR 21 CFR 184.1978 identity text and composition description (URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-02-25/title-21.xml?part=184)
- JECFA CARNAUBA WAX record (CAS 8015-86-9; INS 903) (URL: https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/939)

## Function in Food
- **Primary function:** Glazing/surface-finishing agent.
- **Mechanism of action:** Forms a hydrophobic, hard wax film on food surfaces, improving gloss and acting as a moisture/physical barrier.
- **Other technological functions (regulatory):** Anticaking agent, formulation aid, lubricant/release agent.
- **Common food categories (U.S. GMP uses):** Baked goods/baking mixes, chewing gum, confections/frostings, fresh fruits/fruit juices, gravies/sauces, processed fruits/fruit juices, soft candy.

Evidence:
- Functional classes and ADI page fields in JECFA record (URL: https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/939)

## Key Safety Evidence
### Animal studies
- **1982 short-term rat study (13 weeks):** No treatment-related toxicological findings up to 10% dietary carnauba wax; reported no-untoward-effect level at 10% diet (mean intake about 8.8 g/kg bw/day in males, 10.2 g/kg bw/day in females).
- **1983 subchronic dog study (28 weeks):** No evidence of toxicity/pathology at 0.1%, 0.3%, 1% diet.
- **1983 reproduction/subchronic rat study:** No major adverse findings up to 1% diet, aside from reduced serum free fatty acids at 0.3% and 1%.

Evidence:
- PMID 6890026 (URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6890026/)

### Mechanistic concerns
- A formulation-specific oral nanoparticle paper (carnauba wax-based SLN loaded with rosmarinic acid) reported a safe profile at moderate concentrations but in vitro genotoxic potential at higher test concentration (1.5 mg/mL), mainly via necrosis; this is indirect evidence and not a pure E903 dietary-dose study.

Evidence:

## Exposure Assessment
- **Regulatory benchmark:** EFSA ADI 7 mg/kg bw/day; JECFA ADI 0-7 mg/kg bw.
- **EFSA conclusion on intake vs ADI:** Reported mean and high-level exposure estimates were below ADI across population groups/scenarios in the EFSA re-evaluation summary text indexed from the EFSA opinion record.
- **ADI exceedance risk (general population):** Low based on available regulatory exposure conclusions.
- **Potentially vulnerable groups:** High consumers of heavily glazed/coated foods (e.g., confectionery/chewing gum/polished fruit), though available EFSA summary indicates exposure remained below ADI.

Evidence:
- EFSA opinion URL and indexed summary text context (https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/2880)
- JECFA record (https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/939)

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis
#### risk_free
- **Evidence supporting risk_free:** Natural-origin wax; long history of regulated use; no IARC classification found; older animal studies did not show clear toxic pathology at high dietary concentrations.
- **Evidence against risk_free:** Both EFSA and JECFA use a numeric ADI (7 mg/kg bw/day) rather than "ADI not specified"; modern human long-term dietary datasets specific to E903 are limited.

#### limited
- **Evidence supporting limited:** Approved by EFSA/JECFA/FDA; explicit GMP controls in 21 CFR 184.1978; ADI established and EFSA summary indicates exposure below ADI; no major carcinogenic classification signal from IARC.
- **Evidence against limited:** Much of the core toxicology base is older; some recent mechanistic work is indirect/formulation-specific rather than direct food-additive chronic testing.

#### moderate
- **Evidence supporting moderate:** Residual uncertainty from limited modern chronic human data and reliance on older animal datasets.
- **Evidence against moderate:** No clear regulatory warning trend (no ADI tightening/bans identified here), no IARC hazard classification entry found, and available regulatory exposure conclusions indicate intake below ADI.

#### high
- **Evidence supporting high:** None identified from reviewed sources.
- **Evidence against high:** Not banned in major frameworks reviewed, no IARC Group 2A/2B/1 entry found for carnauba wax, and major regulators maintain approval with ADI/GMP controls.

### 2. Rationale
The strongest signals are consistent regulatory approval with quantitative ADI controls, clear U.S. GMP use restrictions, and historical animal studies without major toxicity at tested doses. Uncertainty remains due to sparse contemporary direct epidemiology for E903 itself and EFSA page accessibility constraints in this environment.

### 3. Recommended tier
**limited**
