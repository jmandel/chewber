# E308 (Gamma-tocopherol) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E308 (gamma-tocopherol) [S1]
- **Primary CAS number identified:** `54-28-4` (PubChem synonym list for gamma-tocopherol) [S11]
- **Chemical class:** Tocopherol (vitamin E isomer; lipophilic phenolic antioxidant) [S1][S11]
- **Common synonyms:** gamma-Tocopherol, (+)-gamma-Tocopherol, D-gamma-Tocopherol, o-Xylotocopherol [S11]
- **Natural vs synthetic:** EFSA evaluated E308 in the same opinion covering natural and synthetic tocopherol forms (d-/dl- context for tocopherols), and eCFR defines commercial tocopherols from vegetable-oil-derived products; market material may therefore include natural-identical and synthetic forms. [S1][S3]

## Function in Food
- **Primary function:** Antioxidant food additive (prevents oxidative deterioration/rancidity in fat-containing foods). [S3]
- **Regulatory function class evidence:** In U.S. regulation, tocopherols are affirmed for use "as antioxidants" in food under GMP conditions. [S3][S4]
- **Common food categories (regulatory/inferred from antioxidant use):** fats/oils and other lipid-containing processed foods; also used in contexts where tocopherols are used as nutrient sources (vitamin E forms). [S1][S3]

## Regulatory Status
- **EFSA (EU):**
  - **Status:** Approved food additive (E308 included in EFSA re-evaluation of E306-E309). [S1]
  - **ADI:** EFSA references a **group ADI of 2 mg/kg bw/day** (expressed as all-rac-alpha-tocopheryl acetate) for tocopherol nutrient-source use; EFSA also concluded no safety concern for reported antioxidant uses/use levels, while noting exposure data limitations for antioxidant-use estimation. [S1]
  - **Evaluation year:** 2015 (EFSA Journal opinion on E306-E309). [S1]
  - **Access note:** EFSA URL resolved to Wiley page that was inaccessible from this VM (Cloudflare challenge), so extraction used EFSA entry text and mirrored metadata paths. [S1][S2]
- **FDA / eCFR (US):**
  - **Status:** GRAS framework applies via 21 CFR Part 182 (Substances Generally Recognized as Safe). Tocopherols are listed at **21 CFR 182.3890**. [S5][S3]
  - **Conditions of use:** Under **21 CFR 182.1(b)(1)**, substances in Part 182 are used in food with no limitation other than current good manufacturing practice; 182.3890 specifies tocopherols used as antioxidants in food. [S4][S3]
- **JECFA / WHO:**

  - Closest additive entry: **Mixed TOCOPHEROLS concentrate (INS 306)** with **ADI 0-2 mg/kg bw**, evaluation year **1973**. [S7][S6]
- **IARC:**
  - No gamma-tocopherol/tocopherol listing was identified in IARC list-of-classifications data checked (current list payload). [S9][S10]
  - **Classification for E308:** no IARC group identified from queried list (treated as not classified in this review). [S10]
- **Notable bans:** No major-jurisdiction ban identified in reviewed EFSA/eCFR/JECFA/IARC sources. [S1][S3][S7][S10]

## Key Safety Evidence
- **Human supplementation safety signal (short-term):** Phase 0 prostate-cancer perioperative supplementation with a gamma-tocopherol-rich vitamin E mixture reported **no adverse events** and no significant estimated blood-loss differences across arms. [S13]
- **Human respiratory challenge trial:** In mild asthmatics, short-course gamma-tocopherol pretreatment did **not** significantly mitigate ozone-induced airway inflammation; this is an efficacy-null result rather than a strong harm signal. [S12]
- **Recent observational signal (renal outcomes):** A CKD cohort analysis (2025) reported a **reversed J-shaped association** between dietary gamma-tocopherol and CKD progression risk, suggesting possible non-linear risk at different intake levels; causality remains unproven. [S14]
- **Mechanistic concern level:** No robust genotoxic/carcinogenic regulatory signal was identified in EFSA/JECFA/IARC sources reviewed. [S1][S7][S10]

## Exposure Assessment
- **Typical dietary intake:** Additive-specific E308 intake estimates were not robustly identified in the reviewed regulatory records; EFSA explicitly noted insufficient data to estimate exposure from antioxidant-use scenarios for E306-E309. [S1]
- **ADI exceedance risk:** Cannot be quantified confidently from available antioxidant-use exposure data in EFSA re-evaluation; risk of exceedance therefore remains uncertain rather than demonstrated. [S1]
- **Potentially vulnerable populations:**
  - Individuals with chronic kidney disease (observational non-linear association data). [S14]
  - High-dose supplement users (general precaution, because dose-response may be non-linear across tocopherol isoforms). [S14][S13]

## Risk Assessment
1. **Tier-by-tier analysis**
- **risk_free**
  - Evidence supporting this tier: E308 is a vitamin E isomer and is regulated/accepted as an antioxidant additive; no IARC carcinogenic classification identified; EFSA did not flag safety concern at reported antioxidant uses. [S1][S3][S10]
  - Evidence against this tier: EFSA had exposure-estimation limitations for antioxidant uses; newer observational data indicate non-linear associations in CKD outcomes, so "no credible harm evidence at all" is too strong. [S1][S14]
- **limited**
  - Evidence supporting this tier: Regulatory acceptance across EFSA/eCFR context; established group ADI frameworks in EFSA/JECFA tocopherol context; no strong human adverse-event signal in short-term supplementation trial. [S1][S3][S7][S13]
  - Evidence against this tier: Exposure uncertainty (EFSA data gap) and possible population-specific non-linear risk signal (CKD cohort) reduce certainty. [S1][S14]
- **moderate**
  - Evidence supporting this tier: Non-linear CKD progression association may indicate caveats in vulnerable populations and suggests intake-pattern sensitivity. [S14]
  - Evidence against this tier: No major regulatory downgrades/bans or IARC 2A/2B signal found; no strong corroborating toxicology/regulatory action indicating broad population harm at approved use. [S1][S3][S10]
- **high**
  - Evidence supporting this tier: No strong evidence found in reviewed authoritative regulatory datasets.
  - Evidence against this tier: No major-jurisdiction ban, no IARC Group 1/2A/2B listing identified, and no EFSA conclusion of inability to confirm safety at reported use levels. [S1][S3][S10]

2. **Rationale**
- The totality of evidence supports an approved additive with established tocopherol ADI context and no major carcinogenic/regulatory red flags, but with non-trivial uncertainty due to exposure-data gaps and recent observational non-linear associations in CKD populations. [S1][S7][S14]

3. **Recommended tier**
- **limited**
