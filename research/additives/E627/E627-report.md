# E627 (Disodium guanylate) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E627 (INS 627)
- **Name:** Disodium 5'-guanylate (disodium guanosine 5'-monophosphate)
- **CAS number(s):** 5550-12-9
- **Chemical class:** Purine nucleotide salt; flavour enhancer (ribonucleotide family)
- **Common synonyms:** Sodium 5'-guanylate, sodium guanylate, GMP
- **Natural vs synthetic:** Naturally occurring nucleotide derivative, but commercial additive supply is typically industrial (e.g., fermentation-derived; see EFSA FEEDAP production description).

**Data extracted:**
- JECFA chemical page for DISODIUM 5'-GUANYLATE (ID 822): chemical name, CAS, synonyms, INS, functional class. [S7]
- EFSA FEEDAP paper on disodium 5'-guanylate states production by fermentation strains (industrial production context). [S12]

## Function in Food
- **Primary function:** Flavour enhancer (umami/savoury enhancement).
- **Mechanism of action:** 5'-ribonucleotides (including GMP) enhance umami and can intensify perceived saltiness in combination with glutamate/salt systems.
- **Common food categories:** General foods as flavour enhancer (regulatory wording), and demonstrated food matrices such as potato products, cheese spread, and beef burgers in sensory studies.

**Data extracted:**
- 21 CFR section text: "may be safely used as a flavor enhancer in foods". [S4]
- PubMed sensory studies (2020, 2021) testing GMP/IMP/MSG effects on salty taste and umami in foods/matrices. [S14], [S15]

## Key Safety Evidence
- **Historic toxicology:** Older toxicology publication exists for disodium 5'-inosinate/guanylate/ribonucleotide (1974). [S13]
- **Recent regulatory safety (feed context):** EFSA FEEDAP (2021) concluded no safety concerns under proposed feed-use conditions for a fermentation-produced disodium 5'-guanylate additive (with reservations specifically about adding via drinking water hygiene conditions). [S12]
- **Human metabolic signal (indirect/high-purine challenge):** A 2024 human proof-of-concept challenge meal included added AMP/GMP/IMP and examined uric acid response in elevated-UA adults; this informs a theoretical uric-acid concern context but is not a standard chronic dietary safety trial for E627 at normal food-use levels. [S11]
- **Non-human/indirect literature exists but is limited for direct human food-additive risk quantification.**

## Exposure Assessment
- **Quantitative population intake for E627 alone:** Not identified in accessible EFSA re-evaluation output (none published yet in the retrieved Open EFSA record) and not provided as numeric intake on JECFA entry page. [S2], [S7]

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis

**risk_free**
- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - JECFA ADI is "NOT SPECIFIED" for disodium 5'-guanylate entry. [S7]
  - No IARC carcinogenic classification identified in current IARC list resources. [S8], [S9], [S10]
- **Evidence arguing against this tier:**
  - EFSA food-additive re-evaluation is still ongoing (no final modern EFSA output in retrieved record). [S2]
  - Recent human evidence includes high-purine challenge contexts relevant to uric-acid physiology, so "no conceivable concern" is too strong. [S11]

**limited**
- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - U.S. CFR explicitly permits use as flavour enhancer under intended-effect limits. [S4]
  - JECFA ADI not specified and longstanding use history. [S7]
  - No direct strong contemporary human harm signal at typical food-additive exposure from retrieved evidence.
- **Evidence arguing against this tier:**
  - Limited modern human dose-response safety dataset specifically for E627 as consumed in foods.

**moderate**
- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - EFSA re-evaluation not yet finalized; uncertainty remains in latest EU reassessment workflow. [S2]
  - Indirect concern for uric-acid-sensitive groups under high-purine challenge scenarios. [S11]
- **Evidence arguing against this tier:**
  - Regulatory bodies (JECFA, FDA CFR use conditions) do not currently indicate broad population harm at intended uses. [S4], [S7]

**high**
- **Evidence supporting this tier:**
  - None identified in retrieved sources (no ban, no IARC 2A/2B/1 classification found, no regulatory withdrawal).
- **Evidence arguing against this tier:**
  - Existing regulatory permissions and JECFA "ADI not specified" position are inconsistent with a high-risk classification. [S4], [S7]

### 2. Rationale
The strongest regulatory signals are permissive (FDA food additive section and JECFA ADI not specified). The main uncertainty driver is that EFSA's food-additive re-evaluation record for E627 remains ongoing with no current adopted output in the retrieved Open EFSA record. Evidence for serious harm at typical use levels is limited and indirect.

### 3. Recommended tier
**`limited`**
