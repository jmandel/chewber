# E154 (Brown FK) Research Report

## Identity
- **E-number:** E154
- **Name:** Brown FK
- **CAS number:** 8062-14-4
- **INS number:** 154
- **Chemical class:** Synthetic **azo dye mixture** (sulfonated aromatic azo compounds)
- **Synonyms:** C.I. Food Brown 1, Brown FK
- **Origin:** Synthetic (prepared by diazotisation/coupling chemistry; not naturally occurring)

## Function in Food
- **Primary function:** Food colour (adds/restores brown colour).
- **Mechanism of action:** Azo chromophores in the dye mixture absorb visible light, shifting product appearance toward brown.
- **Common food categories:** Historically restricted to **kippers/smoked fish** in the EU context described by EFSA.

## Regulatory Status
### EFSA (EU)
- **Latest EFSA re-evaluation:** 2010 (EFSA Journal 8(4):1535).
- **Key finding:** EFSA stated it was **unable to conclude on safety** because of deficiencies in the toxicity dataset.
- **ADI information:** EFSA cited the older SCF ADI of **0.15 mg/kg bw/day** (historical), but did not confirm safety in the 2010 re-evaluation.
- **Status interpretation:** In current EU law, only additives listed in Annex II (Union list) may be used; in the consolidated Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008 text checked, E154/Brown FK was not found.

### FDA / CFR (US)
- **GRAS status:** No evidence of Brown FK being GRAS in Title 21.
- **CFR citation basis:** 21 CFR **§70.3(f)** (color additive definition), **§70.5** (restrictions tied to listing/certification), and listing framework in **Parts 73/74/81**.
- **Observed result from Title 21 XML search:** No matches for `Brown FK`, `Food Brown 1`, `E154`, or `8062-14-4`.
- **Conditions of use:** No Brown FK-specific conditions found in Title 21 listings.

### JECFA / WHO
- **Latest JECFA evaluation year:** 1986.
- **Current JECFA ADI status:** **NO ADI ALLOCATED**.
- **Historical note:** Previous temporary ADI in 1985 was **0-0.075 mg/kg bw/day**.

### IARC
- **Classification:** No Brown FK entry found in IARC’s current “Agents classified” dataset (updated 2026-02-25).

### Notable bans/restrictions
- **EU:** Not identified in the current Union list check (Annex II consolidated text searched for E154/Brown FK).
- **US:** No Title 21 listing found for Brown FK as a permitted color additive.

## Key Safety Evidence
- **Genotoxicity concerns (older data):** Older bacterial and rat-excreta studies report mutagenicity/genotoxicity signals for Brown FK or related metabolites/components.
- **Cardiotoxic/myopathy concerns:** JECFA toxicology text notes some metabolites described as cardiotoxic and reports dose-related myopathy/pigment deposition at higher doses in animal studies.
- **Carcinogenicity evidence quality concerns:** JECFA/EFSA both highlighted limitations in long-term datasets (including incomplete lower-dose histopathology in rat studies), limiting certainty.
- **Human evidence:** JECFA monograph reported no direct human observation dataset (“No information available”).
- **Recent PubMed landscape:** Limited recent safety work; one 2018 paper was analytical (HPLC detection method), not a toxicology reassessment.

## Exposure Assessment
- **EFSA refined exposure estimates (2010):**
  - Children: mean ~0.03-0.07 mg/kg bw/day; 95th percentile up to ~0.14 mg/kg bw/day.
  - Adults: mean ~0.002-0.07 mg/kg bw/day; 95th percentile up to ~0.14 mg/kg bw/day.
- **ADI exceedance context:** Against the historical SCF ADI (0.15 mg/kg bw/day), high-end child exposures were close to the ADI.
- **Potentially vulnerable populations:** Children with higher smoked-fish intake patterns.

## Risk Assessment
### 1. Tier-by-tier analysis
- **risk_free**
  - Evidence supporting: Historically limited permitted use (kippers only) and relatively low modeled average exposure.
  - Evidence against: EFSA could not conclude safety; JECFA latest status is NO ADI ALLOCATED; older genotoxic/cardiotoxic concern signals exist.

- **limited**
  - Evidence supporting: Historical SCF ADI existed; some datasets did not show clear tumour increase.
  - Evidence against: Safety database deficiencies were material enough for EFSA non-conclusion; JECFA removed/withheld ADI at latest status.

- **moderate**
  - Evidence supporting: Exposure in children approached historical ADI at high percentiles; credible animal/mechanistic concerns and data uncertainty.
  - Evidence against: Current regulatory posture appears stricter than a normal “approved with caveats” profile.

- **high**
  - Evidence supporting: EFSA unable to confirm safety; JECFA latest ADI status is NO ADI ALLOCATED; current major-jurisdiction authorization is absent in reviewed EU/US legal listings.
  - Evidence against: No direct IARC carcinogen classification identified; modern human epidemiology is limited.

### 2. Rationale
Primary drivers are: (1) unresolved toxicology uncertainties in authoritative re-evaluations, (2) lack of current positive listing evidence in major jurisdictions reviewed, and (3) historical mutagenicity/cardiotoxicity concerns with incomplete modern confirmation datasets.

### 3. Recommended tier
**high**
