# FDA Regulation of Calcium Phosphates (E341) – 21 CFR Citations

## Research Date: Sourced from eCFR (current as of 2/25/2026, last amended 2/19/2026)

---

## Summary

All three forms of calcium phosphate (E341i, E341ii, E341iii) are classified as **GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe)** by the FDA under 21 CFR Part 182. They appear in multiple subparts covering different functional uses, all with the same condition: safe "when used in accordance with good manufacturing practice" (GMP) — meaning **no specific numerical limits** on usage levels in most applications.

---

## HUMAN FOOD – 21 CFR Part 182 (GRAS Substances)

### 1. 21 CFR §182.1217 – Calcium phosphate (Multiple Purpose GRAS Food Substance)
- **URL**: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-182/subpart-B/section-182.1217
- **Subpart**: B — Multiple Purpose GRAS Food Substances
- **Product**: Calcium phosphate (mono-, di-, and tribasic)
  - This single section covers ALL THREE forms:
    - Monocalcium/monobasic phosphate (E341i)
    - Dicalcium/dibasic phosphate (E341ii)
    - Tricalcium/tribasic phosphate (E341iii)
- **Conditions of use**: "This substance is generally recognized as safe when used in accordance with good manufacturing practice."
- **Limitations**: None specified beyond GMP.

### 2. 21 CFR §182.8217 – Calcium phosphate (Nutrient)

- **Conditions of use**: "This substance is generally recognized as safe when used in accordance with good manufacturing practice."

### 3. 21 CFR §182.6215 – Monobasic calcium phosphate (Sequestrant)

## COLOR ADDITIVE USE – 21 CFR Part 73

### 4. 21 CFR §73.80 – Calcium phosphate (Color Additive)
- **URL**: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73/subpart-A/section-73.80
- **Subpart**: A — Foods (Color Additives Exempt from Certification)
- **Identity**: White, synthetically prepared powder consisting predominantly of precipitated Ca₅OH(PO₄)₃ (hydroxyapatite/tricalcium phosphate form)
- **Specifications**:
  - Loss on ignition: not more than 10%
  - Assay (Ca): 36.0–40.0%
  - Fluoride: not more than 75 mg/kg (75 ppm)
  - Lead: not more than 0.25 mg/kg (0.25 ppm)
  - Arsenic: not more than 3 mg/kg (3 ppm)
- **Uses and restrictions** (SPECIFIC LIMITS for color use):
  - Ready-to-eat chicken products: ≤1.5% by weight of finished food
  - White candy melts: ≤0.25% by weight of finished food
  - Doughnut sugar: ≤2.0% by weight of finished food
  - Sugar for coated candies: ≤5.25% by weight of finished food
- **Certification**: Exempt from batch certification
- **Effective**: 90 FR 20100, May 12, 2025

## KEY FINDINGS

1. **GRAS Status Confirmed**: All three forms of calcium phosphate (mono-, di-, and tribasic) are GRAS under 21 CFR Part 182.

2. **No Specific Usage Limits for GRAS**: When used as a general food substance, nutrient, or sequestrant, the only condition is compliance with "good manufacturing practice" — no maximum percentages or ADI specified in the CFR.

3. **Limits Only for Color Additive Use**: Specific percentage limits exist ONLY under §73.80 when calcium phosphate is used as a color additive (whitening agent).

4. **NOT in Part 184**: Calcium phosphate does NOT appear in 21 CFR Part 184 (Direct Food Substances Affirmed as Generally Recognized as Safe). It remains in Part 182 (the older GRAS list). Part 184 contains more detailed monographs for substances that have been "affirmed" through the more rigorous post-1977 review process.

5. **Multiple Functional Uses Recognized**:
   - Multiple purpose food substance (§182.1217)
   - Nutrient (§182.8217)
   - Sequestrant — monobasic only (§182.6215)
   - Color additive — exempt from certification (§73.80)
   - Prior-sanctioned food-packaging stabilizer (§181.29)

6. **E-Number Mapping**:
   - E341i (Monocalcium phosphate / monobasic) → covered by §182.1217, §182.8217, and §182.6215
   - E341ii (Dicalcium phosphate / dibasic) → covered by §182.1217 and §182.8217
   - E341iii (Tricalcium phosphate / tribasic) → covered by §182.1217 and §182.8217
